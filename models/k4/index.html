Introducing

The K4, in brief.

The K4 is a one-piece white smart toilet with a black display set into the flat top of its rear section and a round side knob ringed with light. It has rear and front wash, a warm air dryer, a heated seat and a deodoriser, adjustable from the remote or the knob.

Why the K4

Choose the K4 if you like a more architectural shape. The inset display keeps the flat top tidy, the rounded bowl softens the outline, and water, air and seat temperature each have four levels.
