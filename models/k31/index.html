Introducing

The K31, in brief.

The K31 is a one-piece white smart toilet with a smooth, rounded lid that follows the line of the body. A small round dial, ringed with blue light, sits in the side of the rear section, and a few small lit icons glow softly on top. It has rear and front wash, a warm air dryer, a heated seat and a deodoriser, adjustable from the remote.

Why the K31

Choose the K31 if you prefer an all-white look. With no dark panel, the lid and body read as one clean shape, and the lit dial and icons are the only accents.
