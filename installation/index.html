Photo guide

Five photos to take.

Take them on your phone in normal light. Landscape or portrait both work. If you can only take a few, start with the first three.

  1. 1

    The whole toilet area, from the doorway

    Stand in the doorway and take in the toilet, the walls around it and the floor.

  2. 2

    The toilet from the side, showing the wall behind

    Step to one side so we can see how the toilet sits against the wall behind it.

  3. 3

    The water outlet / tap (stop valve), close up

    The small tap on the wall or floor that feeds the toilet. Get close so we can see exactly where it is.

  4. 4

    The nearest power point (or the wall where one could go)

    Every model needs a power point near the toilet. If there is none, photograph the wall beside the toilet.

  5. 5

    The floor where the toilet sits

    Show the floor around the base of the toilet.
